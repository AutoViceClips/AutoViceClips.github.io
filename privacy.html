Privacy Policy – ViceClips Uploader

ViceClips Uploader is an internal tool used only by the operators of the YouTube channel ViceClips to upload videos to their own channel via the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The tool requests permission to upload videos and to read basic channel information of the ViceClips channel. It does not access data of any other YouTube users.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operators' own computer and are not shared with third parties. No personal data of viewers is collected, stored or sold.

Third parties

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
